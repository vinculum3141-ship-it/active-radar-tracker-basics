[[slnc 800]] Setup and baseline values. [[slnc 600]]

These introductory lessons use one fixed baseline radar specification. [[slnc 700]]

The carrier frequency is 2.45 gigahertz. This connects the radar to wavelength and, later, Doppler calculations. [[slnc 700]]

The bandwidth is 5 megahertz. This sets the range resolution achievable with pulse compression. [[slnc 700]]

The pulse width is 20 microseconds. This controls how long each burst is transmitted. [[slnc 700]]

The pulse repetition interval, or P R I, is 1 millisecond. This sets how long the radar has to listen before the next pulse. [[slnc 700]]

The sampling rate is 20 megahertz. In physical terms, the receiver records 20 million samples every second. [[slnc 700]]

Finally, the example target is 1,000 meters away. We will use that concrete distance to connect travel time to measured range. [[slnc 1000]]
