First: what is pulse radar? [[slnc 600]]

Pulse radar is a distance sensor built from careful timing. It transmits a short burst of energy toward a scene, then goes quiet and waits for some energy to reflect from a target and return as an echo. Because the radar knows exactly when it transmitted and how fast the energy travels, the returning echo carries a measurement. The whole system is: transmit, listen, and time the return.
